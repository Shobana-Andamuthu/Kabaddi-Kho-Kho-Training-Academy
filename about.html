About Shakti Academy

Twelve years of turning raw talent into disciplined, confident athletes.

Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Built for Tomorrow

Shakti Academy began in 2012 with twelve children and a dusty ground in Coimbatore. Founded by former national kabaddi player Ravi Shankar, it grew into a full academy for both Kabaddi and Kho-Kho.

Today more than 1,200 players have trained with us, and many now represent their districts, states and the country.
