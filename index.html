Training Method

Built Around Performance

Every batch follows a 4-step weekly cycle so players improve in skill, strength and match sense.

  • 1

    Skill Drills

    Position-specific technique for raiders, defenders, chasers and runners.

  • 2

    Strength & Agility

    Core, leg power and reaction work led by a fitness trainer.

  • 3

    Tactics & Review

    Video breakdowns and strategy sessions every Friday.

  • 4

    Match Day

    Weekend practice matches with feedback reports.
