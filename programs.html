FAQ

Common Questions

Do beginners need prior experience?

No. Junior Foundation and our beginner batches start from the basics, and coaches assess each player before placing them in a group.

Can my child join both sports?

Yes. The Both-sport batches and the annual plan let players train in Kabaddi and Kho-Kho.

Is there a free trial class?

Every new player can attend one free trial session. Book it from the Contact page.

How are fees paid?

Fees are paid monthly, quarterly or yearly through your player dashboard, and a receipt is available for every payment.
