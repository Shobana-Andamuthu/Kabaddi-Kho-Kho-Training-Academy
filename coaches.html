Coaching Philosophy

How We Coach

Three principles guide every session.

1

Technique First

Strong fundamentals before speed and power, so players improve safely.

2

Individual Attention

Small batches mean each player gets direct feedback every session.

3

Character Matters

We build respect, resilience and sportsmanship alongside skills.

Player Pathway

From Trial to Tournament

  1. 1

    Assessment

    Coaches evaluate fitness and basic skills on day one.

  2. 2

    Batch Placement

    You join the group that matches your age and level.

  3. 3

    Skill Building

    Weekly drills with monthly progress reports.

  4. 4

    Match Selection

    Top performers are chosen for tournament squads.
